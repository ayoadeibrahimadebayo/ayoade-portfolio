01

Project overview

A clearer route from discovery to enquiry.

TalentSphere was designed around a simple visitor journey: discover a player, review their profile, and contact the manager.

The public website gives scouts and recruiters a place to browse player information without registration. The experience needs to feel credible, easy to scan, and direct enough to turn interest into a conversation.

Project record Role: Product Designer · Duration: 1 month · Platform: responsive web · Tool: Figma.

02

The challenge

Make scattered player information feel structured.

Player information can be difficult to find and compare when details are spread across informal networks and separate sources.

The design needed to help visitors quickly understand a player’s role and key details, while keeping the next step—contacting a manager—clear and accessible.

Design brief Search and filtering should support discovery, cards should enable fast comparison, and profiles should make the enquiry action easy to find.

Designed for

Scouts, clubs, recruiters, agents, and football professionals evaluating emerging talent.

03

Design direction

Lead with a promise, then make the next step obvious.

The interface uses a strong football identity while keeping discovery tools and player information visually distinct.

01 / POSITION

Explain the purpose immediately

The headline “Discover talent. Connect opportunities.” communicates the platform clearly.

02 / GUIDE

Offer two clear paths

“Discover Talent” starts browsing, while “Meet TalentSphere” introduces the platform.

03 / REMOVE FRICTION

Keep registration out of the journey

The public directory is available to visitors without creating an account.

04 / EMPHASISE ACTION

Make enquiry easy to find

Bright green accents highlight primary actions, including the “Contact Manager” next step.

06

Screen exploration

A consistent experience across desktop and mobile.

01 / HOMEPAGE

Make football context feel immediate.

The desktop hero pairs a direct headline with a stadium visual and two clear actions. Supporting copy reinforces that the directory is open and requires no account.

Design decision Keep “Discover Talent” visually dominant while giving “Meet TalentSphere” a quieter but available secondary path.

02 / MOBILE

Stack the message

Navigation condenses while the headline and actions remain prominent.

03 / DISCOVER

Put controls above results

Search and filters sit directly above the scannable player grid.

04 / PLAYER CARD

Lead with identity

Player image, role, key details, and a profile action form one reusable pattern.

05 / CONSISTENCY

Repeat the pattern

A stable card structure helps visitors compare information quickly.

02 / SEARCH

Search by name

A familiar search field gives users a direct route into the directory.

03 / FILTER

Refine the shortlist

Position, age, and nationality filters narrow a broad set of results.

04 / RESULTS

Confirm the effect

A results count makes the outcome of search and filtering visible.

08

Reflection · Next steps

Test the experience against recruiter needs.

DESIGN DECISION

Keep the journey focused.

A public, no-registration flow reduces friction for early exploration and helps visitors reach the player shortlist sooner.

DESIGN DECISION

Make cards predictable.

A repeatable card structure allows position, nationality, age, and profile action to be understood at a glance.

TO VALIDATE

Test search and filters.

Next, observe scouts and recruiters completing realistic discovery tasks and compare which controls help them narrow the directory.

TO VALIDATE

Complete the remaining journey.

Refine full player profile and manager enquiry states based on the information recruiters need to evaluate players and initiate contact.
