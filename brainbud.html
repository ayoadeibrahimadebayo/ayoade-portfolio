01

Project overview

A learning product built around the moments that move study forward.

Brainbud is an educational mobile product exploring digital study tools and an AI-powered tutor for learners preparing for exams.

This case study follows the product direction visible in the supplied screens: a learner can get oriented, open a tutor conversation, practice a question, and review study activity.

Project record My role: Product Designer · Duration: 3 months · Team: Product Manager and Developers. The original brief and research artifacts have not been shared here.

02

The opportunity

Learning support should feel within reach.

Brainbud explores a more connected learning experience: one place to get oriented, work through a question, practice, and find your way back to study.

Learning can involve several kinds of work—understanding a difficult idea, practicing for an exam, and keeping track of what to revisit. This project’s screens explore how those activities might sit together in a mobile product.

Evidence note The materials shared show product screens, not user interviews, research findings, or usability-test results. These are opportunity areas inferred from the interface—not verified findings about learners.

Designed for

Learners preparing for exams and looking for accessible, on-demand study support.

03

The direction

Make study feel like a journey, not a set of tools.

The interface brings a daily learning space, conversational tutoring, exam practice, and progress into a shared mobile experience.

01 / ORIENT

Get set up

Welcome and onboarding screens introduce the learning product and its exam-focused context.

02 / ASK

Work through a question

The tutor entry point offers a conversational starting place, with visible prompts for hints, quizzes, and photo questions.

03 / PRACTICE

Try, respond, review

Practice screens show a question, answer choices, and distinct feedback states that explain the response.

04 / RETURN

Keep learning in view

The home and progress screens surface study activity and ways to pick up a previous task.

08

Key experiences

The AI Tutor, designed as a conversation.

01 / AI TUTOR

Give the learner a way to begin.

The tutor entry screen pairs a chat composer with example starting points: get help with a quadratic, try a quiz, or explain a photo question. Subject and exam chips keep the study context visible.

A note that AI can make mistakes sets an expectation to check important steps. The screen supports a conversational starting point; the supplied assets do not show the tutor’s generated replies, so those are not represented here.

Design consideration Keep suggested prompts optional, preserve context, and make verification guidance visible without interrupting the learning task.

01 / ORIENT

Pick up from home

Study plan, recent work, and a tutor entry point share the learning dashboard.

02 / ASK

Choose a way to start

Prompt suggestions show possible learning actions before the learner types.

03 / TRY

Practice a question

The question interface presents a prompt and a clear set of answer choices.

04 / REVIEW

Understand the response

Feedback uses distinct answer states and explanatory copy to show what to revisit.

The available screens show the tutor entry and practice feedback—not an end-to-end AI conversation. Add a real tutor response screen if you want to tell that part of the story.

02 / EXAM PRACTICE

Make feedback part of the task.

Answer screens show selected options alongside correct or corrective explanations. This connects the response to a reason, rather than only a result.

03 / STUDY CONTINUITY

Make the next step visible.

The learning home and progress overview surface study activity, a plan, and paths back to practice or the tutor.

04 / LEARNER SUPPORT

Consider the wider context.

A parent activation screen is present in the supplied assets. Its broader role and end-to-end behavior need confirmation before a fuller story is added.

10

Design decisions · Learnings

Make the next step feel understandable.

OBSERVATION FROM THE SCREENS

Give learners a way into the tutor.

Suggested prompt types make the AI chat feel like a learning tool with directions, not only an empty text field.

OBSERVATION FROM THE SCREENS

Explain the answer state.

Distinct feedback screens offer space to show why an answer is correct or needs another look. Clear feedback can support understanding.

TO VALIDATE

Check what learners trust.

Test how people interpret AI explanations, when they want hints versus direct guidance, and where confidence reminders belong.

TO VALIDATE

Keep progress meaningful.

Confirm which progress signals help learners plan, and make sure sample values are replaced with real product data before launch.
